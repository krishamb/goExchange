Ashby Run Sheet

Every role here passed your rules: US remote, SF Bay Area hybrid or NYC hybrid with fewer than 3 required office days, no on-site, no investment banks, no companies you excluded, one role per company. Auto-apply fills and submits every form it can answer completely — you only step in for the few it can't (a captcha, or an "are you an AI agent?" question). Set up once below, then press one button.

Simplest: the bookmark (no extension)

Works on any computer, nothing to install. It fills and submits one application per click.

  1. Enter your details

    Type your Gmail and phone below. (Your resume is asked for once, the first time you use it on an Ashby page.)

  2. Drag this to your bookmarks bar

    Drag the green Fill Ashby button up to your bookmarks bar (press Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B to show it first).

    Fill Ashby
  3. On each job: open it, click the bookmark

    Open a job from the list below, then click Fill Ashby. A green “✓ Ashby filler active” bar appears, it fills every answer, and submits if the form is complete. It pauses only for a captcha or an “are you an AI agent?” question.

Power option: Tampermonkey userscript — one click applies to a whole category
  1. Install Tampermonkey

    Chrome, Edge, Firefox. In Chrome/Edge you MUST then open the extension's Details and turn on Allow user scripts — without it the script silently never runs.

  2. Install the filler

    Install Ashby filler — Tampermonkey shows an Install button.

  3. Confirm it works

    Open any Ashby job. You should see the green “✓ Ashby filler active” bar and a panel at bottom right. If you don't, step 1's toggle is off. Then the Auto-apply buttons here work a whole category at a time.
