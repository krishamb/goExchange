Ashby Run Sheet

Every role here passed your rules: US remote, SF Bay Area hybrid or NYC hybrid with fewer than 3 required office days, no on-site, no investment banks, no companies you excluded, at most 3 roles per company. One click per category opens its jobs in one tab. The filler attaches your resume and answers each form; you either let it submit the fully answered ones or click Submit yourself.

Set up once on this computer

  1. Install Tampermonkey

    Free browser extension: Chrome, Edge, Firefox. In Chrome or Edge, open the extension's Details and turn on Allow user scripts.

  2. Install the filler

    Install Ashby filler. Tampermonkey shows an Install button. It updates itself from this repository.

  3. Add your details

    Open any Ashby job. In the filler panel at the bottom right, open Setup: your Gmail address, phone, and your two resumes (architect and executive). They stay in this browser only.

No extension? Use the bookmark instead

It fills one application per click (no batches). Type your Gmail and phone, then drag the green button to your bookmarks bar. The first time you click it on an Ashby page, its panel asks for your resume once.
